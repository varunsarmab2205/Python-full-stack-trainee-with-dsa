Information


Elon Musk

Elon Musk is a businessman and innovator known for leading companies like Tesla and SpaceX. He is famous for advancements in electric vehicles, reusable rockets, and artificial intelligence. Musk has become one of the most influential technology leaders in the world.


Sundar Pichai

Sundar Pichai is the CEO of Google and Alphabet. Born in India, he played a major role in the development of Google Chrome and Android. He is admired for his calm leadership and innovation.


Satya Nadella

Satya Nadella is the CEO of Microsoft. He transformed Microsoft by focusing on cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and collaboration technologies. Under his leadership, Microsoft became one of the world's most valuable companies.


Mark Zuckerberg

Mark Zuckerberg is the co-founder and CEO of Meta Platforms, formerly Facebook. He created Facebook while studying at Harvard University and helped revolutionize social media and virtual reality technologies.
